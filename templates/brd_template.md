# BI Dashboard Business Requirements Document

Template version 1.0. Fill in every section; fields marked **(required)** are needed before dashboard design can start.

## 1. Document Control

### Project name (required)

_Short name of the reporting project._



### Client / business unit (required)

_Customer organisation or business unit that owns the dashboards._



### Industry domain (required)

_ecommerce, retail, finance, BFSI, manufacturing, healthcare, ..._



### Document version

_Version of the customer BRD, e.g. 1.2._



### BRD owner (required)

_Person or role accountable for the requirements._



### Business sponsor (required)

_Executive who sponsors and signs off the project._



### Target BI platform (required)

_Tableau, Power BI, or TBD. The HTML mockup is platform neutral._



## 2. Business Context

### Business objective (required)

_One or two sentences: what decisions the dashboards must support._



### Current state / pain points

_How reporting is done today and what is wrong with it._



### Success criteria (required)

_Measurable outcomes that show the project worked._

_day, week, month, quarter or year._

### Currency & number formats
